# Welcome email — sent after the subscriber confirms (double opt-in)

**Where it goes:** NOT the double opt-in confirmation email (leave MailerLite's stock
"please confirm" alone — it's transactional and gilding it only adds friction). This is the
email that arrives *after* they click confirm. In MailerLite that's an **Automation**:
Automations → the existing "Sage Stage Workflow" → add a **Send email** step after
"Copy to groups", before "Exit flow".

**Subject:** You're in — here's the honest shape of this list

**Preheader:** What you'll get, what you won't, and two links worth having.

---

Hello —

Thanks for confirming. Here's exactly what this list is:

An email when something ships that's worth your minute — new widgets, the desktop app,
workshop resources. And now and then a nudge worth forwarding to a colleague: "have your
staff seen this?"

That's the lot. No weekly anything, quiet over the holidays, and the unsubscribe link
works first time. You picked what you wanted to hear about when you signed up, so you'll
only get the parts you asked for.

Two things worth having while you're here:

**sagestage.app** runs Sage Stage in your browser — no install, no account, and your work
stays on your machine. Three lesson decks build themselves if you want somewhere to start.

**sagestage.co.uk** is the help: every widget explained on one page, the questions teachers
actually ask, and a Your Data page written so you can hand it straight to whoever asks
about GDPR.

Glenn

Sage Stage · HeutaLab
Built by a British educator who still spends time in the classroom.

---

## Notes for whoever edits this next

- Keep it plain text or near it. A newsletter that looks like a brochure gets filed like one.
- The "no weekly anything" promise is load-bearing — it is the reason people stay
  subscribed. Do not break it for a launch.
- MailerLite requires a physical address in the footer (legal, not optional); it inserts
  this automatically from Company profile.
