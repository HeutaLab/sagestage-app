# The nudge — the shape of every update email

Three parts, in this order, never longer than a screen:

1. **The update, plainly.** What shipped, one or two sentences, no adjectives
   the change didn't earn. Link to what's new on the help site.
2. **The staffroom nudge.** One concrete thing worth showing a colleague,
   written so it can be forwarded as-is — "…but have your staff seen the story
   map? A child offers a word, it's in the bank and scored before the moment
   passes. Two minutes in the taster: sagestage.app"
3. **The quiet close.** "That's everything this time." Sign-off, done.
