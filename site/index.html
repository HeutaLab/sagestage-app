Made by a UK primary teacher: clocks, timers, name pickers, rekenreks, base-10, story maps, word banks — about fifty widgets that behave like classroom equipment, not like a website. No accounts. No tracking. Nothing leaves your machine.

Try it now — in your browser Download for macOS — free beta

The taster runs entirely in this browser tab and keeps your work in this browser's storage — honest but modest. The desktop app keeps decks in a real file in your Documents, with two weeks of daily backups. Nothing made in the taster is hostage: export it, import it on desktop.
