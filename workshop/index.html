The Human in the Middle

Some classroom AI closes the loop child → AI → child, and the teacher becomes the meat between the student and the screen. This workshop is about the other topology: a human at every joint where meaning happens — the teacher witnesses, a human evaluates, the output flows home — and the AI as the typist at the edge, never the judge in the centre.

Slides, materials and the tools shown in the session will appear on this page after it runs. In the meantime, the taster is open — everything it does stays on the machine in front of you, which is rather the point.
