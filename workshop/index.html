The Human in the Middle

This workshop is about building with AI while accountability stays on the human side. Some classroom tools close the loop child → AI → child, and the teacher becomes the meat between the student and the screen — the machine quietly making decisions that belong to a person. The other topology keeps a human answerable at every joint: the teacher witnesses, a human evaluates, the output flows home. The AI is the typist at the edge, never the judge in the centre, and never doing your bidding on a decision you own.

Slides, materials and the tools shown in the session will appear on this page after it runs. In the meantime, the taster is open — everything it does stays on the machine in front of you, which is rather the point.
